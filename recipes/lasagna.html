Lasagna Recipe

Description

ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar pasta sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta
  • 3 cups shredded mozerella

Steps

  1. Boil noodles, then drain.
  2. Brown the beef and stir in the sauce.
  3. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella in a 9x13 dish. Repeat.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes.
  5. Rest 15 minutes, then serve.
